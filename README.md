# Sports Club Management System (Karate & Gymnastics)

A comprehensive Sports Club Management System specifically designed for the club manager to streamline daily operations in athletic facilities specializing exclusively in two core disciplines: Karate and Gymnastics.

## Objective

The primary objective of this application is to replace traditional manual paperwork with an organized, digital, and efficient workflow that gives the club manager complete visibility and control over all administrative tasks.

## Core Modules

The system integrates five essential modules into a single unified platform:

1. Players Management Module
   - Maintains organized digital profiles for all club members.
   - Allows the manager to easily record, update, and track essential personal information and contact details within either the Karate or Gymnastics department.

2. Coaches Management Module
   - Organizes instructor records by assigning each coach to their respective sport department.
   - Tracks coach specializations and manages their assigned training groups.

3. Subscriptions and Billing Module
   - Handles membership plans, monthly or seasonal renewals, and payment statuses.
   - Provides automated tracking of active and expired subscriptions to ensure accurate financial oversight.

4. Schedules and Timetables Module
   - Allows the club manager to create, update, and coordinate daily and weekly training sessions for both Karate dojos and Gymnastics halls without any time conflicts.

5. Equipment and Inventory Module
   - Monitors the availability, condition, and maintenance needs of essential training gear, such as gymnastics mats, safety pads, karate punching bags, and protective equipment.

## Summary

Overall, this system enhances the club manager's productivity, minimizes scheduling and administrative errors, and provides a reliable digital foundation for running a specialized Karate and Gymnastics club.
